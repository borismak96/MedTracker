English

What PillPal is

PillPal is designed to help you easily track your daily medications and health data. It is a simple, intuitive, and privacy-respecting tool to make managing your health easier.

You can keep a medication list, set reminder times, log doses as taken or skipped, and optionally record mood, notes, blood pressure, and heart rate. A Medical Card view and PDF export are available for your own reference.

This app is a personal health tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide professional medical diagnoses or advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.

藥伴伴是什麼

藥伴伴旨在幫助您輕鬆記錄日常服藥情況與健康數據。這是一個簡單、直觀且尊重隱私的追蹤工具，讓您的健康管理更加輕鬆。

你可以建立藥物清單、設定提醒時間、將劑量標記為已服用或已跳過，並可選擇記錄心情、備註、血壓與心率。應用程式亦提供醫療卡檢視與 PDF 匯出，供你個人參考。

本應用程式僅作為個人健康記錄與追蹤工具。並非醫療器材，亦不提供任何專業的醫療診斷或建議。若您有任何醫療疑問，請務必諮詢專業醫師。

Privacy at a glance

Health and medication data you enter stays on your iPhone. PillPal does not collect, sell, or share your sensitive health data with third parties. Backups follow your iPhone settings. See the full Privacy Policy.

隱私重點

你輸入的健康與藥物資料會留在你的 iPhone 上。藥伴伴不會收集、出售或與第三方分享你的敏感健康資料。備份方式依你的 iPhone 設定而定。詳見完整隱私權政策。
